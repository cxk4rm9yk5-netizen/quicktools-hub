Introduction

QuickTools Hub provides free online calculators and converters. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website.

Information You Enter

The age, percentage, date, and unit calculators perform calculations in your browser. The current version does not require an account, and these tools are designed to process the values you enter on your device.

The currency converter sends a request to an external exchange-rate service to retrieve rates. That service may receive technical information associated with your request, such as your IP address. Please review the service provider's own privacy policy for details.

Cookies and Advertising

QuickTools Hub does not currently intentionally set advertising cookies. If advertising or analytics services are added in the future, they may use cookies or similar technologies, and this policy should be updated to explain those practices.

Third-party services, including our hosting provider, may process technical data as part of delivering the website.

Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The currency converter uses an external exchange-rate API. These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our tools are intended for general use and are not designed to collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take a simple, privacy-conscious approach to our tools. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the website changes. The date at the top will be revised when appropriate.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use the contact details provided on our website once available.
